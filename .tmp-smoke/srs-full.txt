 1. Introduction

 1.1 Purpose

  This document specifies the software requirements for the Human Resource
  Management System (HRMS) v1 that NexAI Analytics is building for Qii Creative. It is
  written for Qii's management (Asman, Fayaz) to sign off scope, and for the design,
  development and test team to build and verify against.

 1.2 Scope

  HRMS v1 is a web application, usable on phone and desktop, that replaces spreadsheets
  and ClickUp-based HR tracking for Qii's Bangladesh and Australia teams. It covers:

      Sign-in with Google and role-based access to every page and action
      Organisation structure, reporting hierarchy and employee records with documents
      Work schedules (5-day, 6-day, custom and hourly), daily check-in and check-out, and
      worked hours
      Tasks and mandatory daily work logs
      Leave categories, balances, multi-step approval and a per-team daily leave limit
      Salary records and monthly payroll calculated from attendance and leave
      Holiday calendars, onboarding and offboarding checklists
      In-app and email notifications, including a monthly attendance report emailed to every
      employee
      An admin dashboard, exportable reports and an audit log

                 leave covering it

                 task on a given day

Working day      A day in the employee's schedule that is not a
                 holiday on their location's calendar

Worked hours     Check-out time minus check-in time for a day

1.5 Conventions

    Each requirement has an ID: FR (functional), BR (business rule), NFR (non-functional),
    DR (data), IR (interface).
    Must means required for go-live. Should means planned for v1 but can slip to v1.1 with
    Qii's agreement.
    Figures shown as examples (hours, quotas, salaries) are admin settings, not fixed
    values.

2. Overall description

2.1 Product perspective

HRMS v1 is the first release of a single Qii platform. The project-management release that
follows will reuse the same employee, team and role records, replace the stand-in task list
with client projects, and add KPI scoring and bonus. HRMS v1 must therefore keep the
employee table, roles and tasks in a shape the project module can extend.

2.3 Operating environment

    A responsive web application for current Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on desktop,
    and Chrome and Safari on Android and iOS phones
    Hosted in the cloud with HTTPS only; data stored in one region chosen with Qii
    Two locations and time zones at launch: Bangladesh (Asia/Dhaka) and Australia
    (Australia/Darwin)
    Two pay currencies at launch: BDT and AUD

2.4 Design and implementation constraints

    Sign-in only through Google accounts on the qiicreative.com.au domain
    Every policy value (quotas, hours, limits, rules) is admin-editable; none is hard-coded
    No money penalties for lateness in v1
    Payroll calculates gross pay and attendance-based deductions only; tax and statutory
    items are out of scope

2.5 Assumptions and dependencies

    All staff, including contractors, have an @qiicreative.com.au Google account
    Qii's admin enters employees, schedules, holidays, leave categories, opening balances
    and salaries before go-live
    Qii provides a sending address and email service for notifications and monthly reports
    Employees have a phone or computer with a browser on every working day
